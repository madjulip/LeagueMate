Welcome to LeagueMate!

LeagueMate generates a custom schedule specifically for dart leagues. No account or installation is needed. Simply save this link for later, and schedule to your heart's content.

  1. Choose your schedule. Start with one schedule, or click “Add Another League” if you have two independent leagues. Switch between them using the tabs. Each league has its own settings, team names, and schedule.
  2. Enter your league information. Fill in how many teams you have, how many days you play each week, how many dartboards are available each day, and how many weeks the season lasts.
  3. Choose your playing days. Enter the season start date if you know it or keep it blank. In “Playing days,” add the list of days your league plans matches, separated by commas (for example: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday).
  4. Enter your team names. Replace Team 1, Team 2, and so on with the actual names.
  5. Click “Create My Schedule.” Wait for the schedule to appear. This may take a moment.
  6. Review your schedule. Choose a week and day to see the teams, boards, and matches for each round. “Byes” are teams that have the week off.
  7. Download your schedule. After creating your schedule, use Step 3 to download a spreadsheet or JPG schedules. For JPGs, choose the entire season, selected week, or selected day, then click the JPG download button. Multiple JPGs are bundled in a ZIP file.
  8. Save your work (optional). “Save My Settings” saves in this browser. The optional gray backup buttons save a copy of your league information and schedule. For example, if you want to share/view a schedule as it appears on your browser on a separate device, click “Export League Backup” to download a backup file, then use “Import League Backup” on the other device or browser. Note: this is not the same thing as downloading the spreadsheet schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I create a schedule? Each board needs two teams, and each team plays only one day per week. For example, 8 teams can play 1 day per week using 4 boards. If you have too few teams, reduce the number of days or boards.

Will my schedule be here tomorrow? “Save My Settings” keeps your settings and schedule in this browser; it does not download a file. Your work will usually still be available when you return using the same browser. Use “Export League Backup” for extra protection.

Can I make changes? Yes. Changing league settings means you will need to create a new schedule. Save a league backup first if you want to keep the old one.

What are “Schedule Checks” and “Opponent History”? These are optional reports. You do not need them to make or print your schedule.
