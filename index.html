Welcome to LeagueMate!

LeagueMate generates a custom schedule specifically for dart leagues. No account or installation is needed. Simply save this link for later, and schedule to your heart's content.

  1. Enter your league information. Fill in how many teams you have, how many nights you play each week, how many dartboards are available each night, and how many weeks the season lasts.
  2. Choose your playing days. Enter the season start date if you know it or keep it blank. In “Playing days,” type the days your league plays, separated by commas (for example: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday).
  3. Enter your team names. Replace Team 1, Team 2, and so on with the actual names.
  4. Click “Create My Schedule.” Wait for the schedule to appear. This may take a moment.
  5. Review your schedule. Choose a week and day to see the teams, boards, and matches for each round. “Byes” are teams that have the week off.
  6. Download your schedule. After creating your schedule, scroll down to the dark green “Download Schedule (Spreadsheet)” or “Print or Save as PDF” buttons.
  7. Save your work (optional). “Save My Settings” saves in this browser. The optional gray backup buttons will save a copy of your current view. For example, if you want to share a schedule as it appears in your browser or view it on a separate device, "Export League Backup" to save the version, and then "Import League Backup" on another device or browser. Note this will not download the spreadsheet schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I create a schedule? Each board needs two teams, and each team plays only one night per week. For example, 8 teams can play 1 night per week using 4 boards. If you have too few teams, reduce the number of nights or boards.

Will my schedule be here tomorrow? “Save My Settings” keeps your settings and schedule in this browser; it does not download a file. Your work will usually still be available when you return using the same browser. Use “Export League Backup” for extra protection.

Can I make changes? Yes. Changing league settings means you will need to create a new schedule. Save a league backup first if you want to keep the old one.

What are “Schedule Checks” and “Opponent History”? These are optional reports. You do not need them to make or print your schedule.
